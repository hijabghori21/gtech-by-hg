Everything starts with understanding.

Explore common questions about websites, design, software projects and getting in touch. If you need more information, contact us directly.

What does G.TECH Technologies do?
G.TECH's website presents digital service areas such as web development, UI/UX design, software solutions and AI-related concepts. Contact the team to confirm currently available services.
What types of websites can be created?
Website projects can include business websites, portfolios, landing pages and other web experiences. The best solution depends on your goals, features and technical requirements.
Can I request a custom software solution?
You can share your software idea, the problem you want to solve and the features you need. The scope, feasibility and availability should be discussed before a project is confirmed.
How does a project usually begin?
A typical starting point is a discussion about your goals, audience, requirements and timeline. The next steps can then include planning, design, development and testing, depending on the project.
How much does a website or app cost?
Cost depends on the project scope, design, functionality, integrations and maintenance needs. Share your requirements through the contact page to discuss an estimate.
How long does a project take?
Timelines vary according to complexity, the number of features, content availability and review cycles. A realistic timeline should be agreed after the requirements are understood.
Can I request changes during development?
Project changes can be discussed with the team. Their effect on the schedule, scope and cost depends on the agreed project process.
Does G.TECH offer internships?
The Careers page describes example career paths, but it does not confirm current vacancies. Contact the team to ask about actual internship openings and application requirements. Visit Careers for more.
How can I discuss my project idea?
Visit the Contact page and share your project goals, preferred features and any important requirements. The available contact options depend on the details configured for the website.
Will my website work on mobile devices?
Responsive design helps a website adapt to different screen sizes. Mobile compatibility should be checked across relevant devices and browsers before launch.
